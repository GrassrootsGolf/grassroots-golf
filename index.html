About

Grassroots Golf Co. helps people become more comfortable and confident golfers. A golfer is someone who can confidently enjoy golf anywhere.

We work with beginners, returning golfers, higher handicappers, and anyone who wants more confidence and understanding on the course.

We meet people where they are. Sessions focus on building confidence through practical experience, clear explanations, and manageable learning. We complement PGA professionals, clubs and ranges — we do not replace them.

Based at: Golf Paradise, 186 Mona Vale Road, St Ives NSW 2075

Programs

One-on-one coaching

Personalised sessions focused on your goals, movement, and confidence. We observe first, then introduce one or two useful things you can actually use.

Small group coaching

Relaxed small groups for beginners, returning golfers and those looking for more confidence. Clear explanations, no assumed knowledge.

Guided 9-hole on-course rounds

Real golf on real courses. No pressure. No score required. We cover the flow of the round, etiquette, decisions, and building confidence.
$30 Grassroots fee + course green fee. Groups of 3–4.

Simulator sessions

Fundamentals and practice in a controlled environment at Golf Paradise. Useful for building familiarity before or alongside on-course work.

You do not need a low handicap. You do not need to know every rule. We don’t tell people what they should already know.
