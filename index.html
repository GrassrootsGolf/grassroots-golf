Grassroots Golf Co.

Helping beginners become golfers.

Most golfers are taught how to swing. Very few are taught how to play golf.

Grassroots helps you learn about the game and yourself, put that into practice and feel confident enjoying golf anywhere.

Learn. Understand. Enjoy.

Golf is more than learning to swing. Learn about the game and about yourself. Then put that into practice so it starts to make sense. And enjoy piecing it all together.

Learn

Golf is more than the swing. It’s also knowing the game, making decisions and handling how you feel out there.

Understand

Knowing something isn’t the same as being able to use it. Learn how and when to put it to work.

Enjoy

Golf never goes to plan, and that’s okay. Stay curious, make smarter choices and enjoy the progress, not just the score.

See how it works

It’s not complicated. It’s just rarely taught.

Becoming a golfer isn’t about a perfect swing. It’s knowing how to get around the course, choose your shots, set fair expectations and still enjoy it when things don’t go to plan.

We believe a golfer is someone who can confidently enjoy golf anywhere.
