Who we help, and how

We work with beginners, returning golfers, higher handicappers, and anyone who wants more confidence and understanding on the course.

Guided Golf

Real golf on real courses. No pressure. No score required. We cover the flow of the round, etiquette, decisions, and building confidence.

Pricing on request

About Guided Golf

Simulator & Practice

Fundamentals and practice in a controlled environment at Golf Paradise, St Ives. Useful for building familiarity before or alongside on-course work.

Pricing on request

About Simulator & Practice

Personalised Coaching

Sessions focused on your goals, movement and confidence — one-on-one or in a relaxed small group. We observe first, then introduce one or two useful things you can actually use.

About Personalised Coaching

Learn. Understand. Enjoy.

Knowledge becomes wisdom when you take it onto the course.

Learn

The skills and knowledge that help you participate — about yourself, the swing and the game.

Understand

Apply that knowledge in real situations so you can make better decisions, and so it becomes wisdom.

Enjoy

The never-ending process of learning, so golf feels accessible and sustainable.

We complement PGA professionals

We meet people where they are. Sessions focus on building confidence through practical experience, clear explanations and manageable learning. We work alongside PGA professionals, clubs and ranges — we do not replace them.
