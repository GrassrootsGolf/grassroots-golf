Guided Golf

Take your golf onto a real course with someone beside you. We’ll help with real shots, choices and situations, plus how a round flows, pace, safety, etiquette, where to aim and what to do when a shot doesn’t go to plan.

Ask about this program

Golf for children through play.

Golf Quest

Kids golf arcade. Targets to knock down. Points to chase. Personal bests to beat.

Kids think they’re playing games. We’re building confidence, coordination and a love of sport.

  • All primary ages
  • 6–10 kids
  • 2-hour sessions
  • Weekly during term
  • No experience needed

One rule: have fun safely. Kids get to whack balls in a space that is all their own, while friends cheer them on from designated non-hitting areas. Age-appropriate equipment. Small groups. Clear zones. Supervised sessions.

Every child receives their own Golf Quest Scorecard to track scores, celebrate achievements, earn stars and chase a new personal best every week.

We supply the equipment and set up the activities. Runs on an oval, hall or covered area (subject to site assessment). WWCC, insurance and documentation available.

Certified & qualified: MyGolf | Get Into Golf | TPI Level 1.

Different golfers need different places to learn. Depending on where you’re at, sessions might be on a simulator, practice area or golf course, one-on-one or in a small group. We also bring golf to you: home, backyard, workplace, community or events.
