Programs

A clear way to start, practise and play.

You do not need a low handicap. You do not need to know every rule. We don’t tell beginners what they should already know.

Guided Golf

Real golf on real courses. No pressure. No score required.

Guided rounds are a supported way for beginner, developing and returning golfers to get out and actually play. We cover the flow of the round, etiquette, decisions and building confidence. This is not nine holes of constant swing instruction. The swing matters, but the focus is learning how to play golf.

Groups are generally three to four golfers, arranged at suitable courses across Sydney.

Simulator & Practice

Fundamentals and practice in a controlled environment at Golf Paradise, St Ives.

A useful way to build familiarity and confidence before or alongside on-course work. Sessions are practical and beginner-friendly, with clear explanations and no assumed knowledge.

Personalised Coaching

Sessions focused on your goals, movement and confidence.

Choose one-on-one coaching or a relaxed small group. We observe first, then introduce one or two useful things you can actually use. We meet people where they are, with manageable learning and clear explanations.
