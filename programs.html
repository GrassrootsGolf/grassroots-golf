Beginner-to-Golfer

The overarching program. Learn the basics of the game, not just the swing. Build practical knowledge, simple skills and confidence so you can get out and start playing on your own. Internal levels take you through wherever you are.

Guided Golf sits inside this as a practical entry point and one-off option.

2 hours. Pricing on request.

I can hit a ball. I want help actually playing.

Guided Golf

Take your golf onto a real course with someone beside you. We’ll help with real shots, choices and situations, plus how a round flows, pace, safety, etiquette, where to aim and what to do when a shot doesn’t go to plan.

Group Guided Golf: $70 per person, green fee included. 2 hours, groups of 3–4.

I already play. I want to understand my golf better.

Understand Your Game

Get to know your tendencies, decisions and expectations, and what matters most for your game. Rather than chasing another swing fix, we’ll connect your technique to you and the situation.

2 hours. Pricing on request.

Golf for children through play.

Golf Quest

Kids golf arcade. Targets to knock down. Points to chase. Personal bests to beat.

Kids think they’re playing games. We’re building confidence, coordination and a love of sport.

  • All primary ages
  • 6–10 kids
  • 2-hour sessions
  • Weekly during term
  • No experience needed

One rule: have fun safely. Kids get to whack balls in a space that is all their own, while friends cheer them on from designated non-hitting areas. Age-appropriate equipment. Small groups. Clear zones. Supervised sessions.

Every child receives their own Golf Quest Scorecard to track scores, celebrate achievements, earn stars and chase a new personal best every week.

We supply the equipment and set up the activities. Runs on an oval, hall or covered area (subject to site assessment). WWCC and documentation available.

Certified & qualified: MyGolf | Get Into Golf | TPI Level 1.

2 hours. Pricing on request.

How Grassroots can work with you

Different golfers need different places to learn. Depending on where you’re at, sessions might be on a simulator, practice area or golf course, one-on-one or in a small group. We also bring golf to you: home, backyard, workplace, community or events.

We’ll recommend what suits you best.

Private sessions

  • Private Simulator Session: $160 (normally $220) — 2 hours one-on-one + simulator hire at St Ives (+$40 per extra person)
  • TPI-Integrated Private Golf Development: $180 (normally $300) — 2-hour private simulator + TPI screening + personalised development plan
