About

Helping beginners become golfers.

A golfer is someone who can confidently enjoy golf anywhere.

Grassroots Golf Co. helps people become more comfortable and confident golfers. We work with beginners, returning golfers, higher handicappers, and anyone who wants more confidence and understanding on the course.

We meet people where they are. Sessions focus on building confidence through practical experience, clear explanations and manageable learning.

Our approach

Learn. Understand. Enjoy. We help you learn the skills and knowledge that help you participate; understand why things work so you can make better decisions in real situations; and enjoy the never-ending process of learning, so golf feels accessible and sustainable.

Because golf is more than just a swing. Knowledge becomes wisdom when you take it onto the course.

We complement PGA professionals, clubs and ranges — we do not replace them. We don’t tell beginners what they should already know.

Where we’re based

Golf Paradise, 186 Mona Vale Road, St Ives NSW 2075. Guided Golf also runs at suitable courses across Sydney.

Questions we often hear

Do I need to be a beginner?

No. We work with beginners, returning golfers, and people who already play but want more confidence and understanding on the course.

Is this suitable if I get nervous on the course?

Yes. A big part of what we do is helping golf feel less intimidating. The environment is practical, supportive and low pressure.

Will we keep score?

Only if it helps you. The focus is learning, confidence and enjoyment — not chasing a number.

Do I need clubs?

Bring them if you have them. If you don’t, mention it when you get in touch.

How is this different from a normal golf lesson?

The focus is comfortable playing: decisions, etiquette, expectations and real situations — not just the swing.

Can I come with a friend?

Yes. Guided Golf works well with small groups, and you are welcome to invite a friend. They should register too so their place can be accounted for.
