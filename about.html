The Grassroots idea

A golfer is someone who can confidently enjoy golf anywhere.

Most golfers are taught how to swing. Very few are taught how to play golf. Grassroots is here to help fill that gap.

How Grassroots started

Tim grew up playing golf through juniors, cadets and lessons with PGA professionals. He learned plenty about technique, but still saw the same gap: golfers are taught how to swing, not how to play, score, understand and enjoy the game.

Grassroots Golf Co. gives beginners and everyday golfers a welcoming, practical way to feel more comfortable, capable and confident on the course.

Tim Chee — TPI Certified Level 1, TPI Level 2 Golf. National deliverer of MyGolf and Get Into Golf.

Learn. Understand. Enjoy.

Golf is more than learning to swing. Learn about the game and about yourself. Then put that into practice so it starts to make sense. And enjoy piecing it all together.

Learn

Golf is more than the swing. It’s knowledge, decisions and emotion too. Learn how a hole is designed, what your handicap means and what to expect. Then learn your own tendencies, what you can do and how you react.

Example: When the ball is above your feet, it tends to go left for a right-hander. That’s the knowledge.

Understand

Knowing something doesn’t mean you can use it. Understanding is knowing how and when to use what you’ve learned, for the shot in front of you and the golfer you are that day.

Example: The ball is above your feet and there’s trouble left. You know the slope will pull it left, so you adjust your aim. You’re not just knowing it any more. You’re using it.

Enjoy

Golf never goes exactly to plan. Your swing, body, confidence and results change day to day. That’s part of the game. Enjoyment comes from curiosity, better decisions and progress beyond the score.

That’s the game within the game. Learn. Understand. Enjoy. Repeat.

Golf is mental. It is also emotional.

Knowing the smart shot does not guarantee that you will play it.

You can know that aiming at the flag brings a bunker into play and still fire straight at it. You can know one bad hole doesn’t matter and still carry it to the next tee.

Learning your golf means learning how you react, what you expect and how you decide.

Practical support for the whole golfer

Grassroots works alongside PGA professionals, clubs and ranges. It doesn’t replace them. We help you connect your technique, knowledge, decisions and confidence to real situations on the course.

The purpose of Grassroots is to remove barriers to coaching. At Grassroots, we come to you. Physical base with easy access: Golf Paradise, 186 Mona Vale Road, St Ives NSW 2075. We also bring golf to your home, backyard, workplace, community or events.

Questions we often hear

Do I need to be a beginner?

No. We work with beginners, returning golfers, and people who already play but want more confidence and understanding on the course.

Is this suitable if I get nervous on the course?

Yes. A big part of what we do is helping golf feel less intimidating. The environment is practical, supportive and low pressure.

Will we keep score?

Only if it helps you. The focus is learning, confidence and enjoyment — not chasing a number.

Do I need clubs?

Bring them if you have them. If you don’t, mention it when you get in touch.

How is this different from a normal golf lesson?

The focus is comfortable playing: decisions, etiquette, expectations and real situations — not just the swing. We complement PGA professionals; we do not replace them.

Can I come with a friend?

Yes. Group Guided Golf is for groups of 3–4, and you are welcome to invite a friend. They should get in touch too, so their place can be accounted for.

How long is a session?

Sessions are 2 hours.
